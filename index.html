Minnesota

The state of Minnesota is often thought of as either freezing, or covered in mosquitos. And those descriptions are correct! Minnesota is often extremely cold, with temps dropping to -35 degrees fahrenheit, and that's WITHOUT windchill. But when the ice freezes around the tree branches, it's a true winter wonderland to behold. In the summer, the mosquitos can absolutely swarm you. But the amount of green surrounding you as well will take your breath away. Minnesota also has beautiful falls and hopeful springs. It is home to working lighthouses, The North Shore, and of course, 10,000+ lakes!
